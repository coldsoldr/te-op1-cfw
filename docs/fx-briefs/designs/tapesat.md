## What it is

The firmware's composition without its box, spread over the width on the family's rows (the kit's
8-unit grid where the family allows): a **blue input-gain gauge** at x 16 (rows 28..124, 0 dB at the
centre mark y 76, the bar up for + and down for -, the bitcrusher's gauge in the colour of the knob
that drives it), the **two reels** r 30 at (96,48) and (208,48) with their green hubs, the tape path
off the reels' outer edges down to the **rail** at y 100 carrying the blue saturation wave between the
reels, the dim **head** under the gap, the **wow -> flutter scale** on y 124 (the gauges' foot row,
x 100..204 with the slow and fast glyphs outside it), the white **low-pass glyph** at the right centred
on the gauges' centre row (y 64..88) with the hiss specks above it, and the orange **mix gauge** at
x 304. Blue pressed: a bracket round the gain gauge and a blue dB readout at the top left.

## Ids and mappings

| id | kind | value → |
|---|---|---|
| `sat0..sat7` | variant set | saturate: k = round(7 x s); the wave y = 100 - 8 tanh(g sin) / tanh(g), g = 0.6 x 1.45^k, 2 cycles over x 126..178 |
| `reelL`, `reelR` | groups, translate | per frame: up to 2 x wobble px at 0.5 Hz (wow) .. 8 Hz (flutter), the right a quarter turn behind (as the firmware's) |
| `wobble` | line, translate | wobble: `translate(104 x w px, 0)`, x 100 (wow) .. 204 (flutter) |
| `agef` | line, ScaleX about 248 | age: the flat 248..276 scaled by 1 - 22a/28 (the corner walks to 254) |
| `ages` | polyline, translate | age: the slope (276,64)-(288,88) moved by -22a px with the corner |
| `agef`, `ages` | line colour | age: `palwhite` -> half way to `palmid` #514959 |
| `age0..age5` | count set | round(6 x age) specks shown |

Age here is the wear, 32767 - the white knob's word: white turns the stock way, clockwise = a newer
tape (the user, 2026-10-27), in the render and on the screen alike.
| `gbar` | rect, matrix | gain word 5: scaled -1..1 about y 76 (`matrix(1,0,0,d,0,76(1-d))`, d = (w - 16384) / 16384) |
| `gzero` | static | the 0 dB mark |
| `gbox`, `gs0..1`, `gd0..6`, `gdot`, `gt0..9` | shown on page 2 | the bracket and the readout: tenths = (w x 120 + 16383) / 32767 - 60 |
| `mbar` | rect, matrix | mix: scaled about its foot (y 124) |
| `palette` | hidden lines | the colours the screen blends between |

Static group `static`: reels, tape path, head, the scale's track and glyphs, the gauge frames.

## Gain in the core

`TapeParams::gain_oct` (Q16 octaves, default 0; the plugin passes (word - 16384) x 4, about -6..+6 dB)
adds to the saturation drive's exponent e = -2 + 6s, clamped to the knob's own range -2..4, so at
either end of the saturate knob the gain adds nothing. The level make-up table is indexed by the knob
alone, so the gain is heard as more or less saturation, not level, and the dry side of the mix never
sees it. `ports/fx/tests/test_tape.cc` passes unchanged on the host.

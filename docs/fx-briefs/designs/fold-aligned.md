## What it is

The firmware's composition without its box, on the family's rows: the **blue input-gain gauge** at
x 16 (rows 28..124, 0 dB at y 76, the bar up for + and down for -), **one output cycle** drawn large
over x 44..236 with amplitude 36 about y 76 between two dim fold lines at y 40 and 112, the **green
offset line** on y 76 sliding +-24 px with symmetry, the white **low-pass glyph** at the right centred
on y 76 (x 248..288, as tapesat's), and the orange **mix gauge** at x 304. Blue pressed: a bracket round
the gain gauge and a blue dB readout at the top left.

## Gain in the core

`FoldParams::gain` (Q14, default 16384) scales the folder's gain from the fold knob's table, kept
within the knob's own range (0.25..4 turns), so at either end of the knob the gain adds nothing. The
level make-up table is indexed by the knob alone, so the gain is heard as more or fewer folds, not
level, and the dry side of the mix never sees it. The gain is the fifth smoothed knob in `Fold::Control`.
`ports/fx/tests/test_fold.cc` passes unchanged on the host.
